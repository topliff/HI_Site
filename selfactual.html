Heliotrope Imaginal runs the AllStarTeams and Imaginal Agility workshops. We partner with selfActual to keep your workshop results in a private vault that you own. This page explains what each of us does and what that means for your data.

Who does what

  • Heliotrope Imaginal designs and runs the workshops: the steps, your Star Card, your reflections, your reports, and your facilitator's view of your progress.
  • selfActual is our technology partner. It provides sign-in, your personal data vault, and some of the AI features in the workshops.

Signing in

When you log in to the workshops you'll see a sign-in screen run by selfActual. Your account is created from your Heliotrope Imaginal invitation, and you use the same login each time you come back. The first time you connect, you may be asked to allow Heliotrope Imaginal to access your selfActual account. That's what lets your results reach your vault.

Your vault

When you register you can set up a personal selfActual vault. It belongs to you. selfActual holds it for you as custodian, on its own infrastructure, under its own Terms and Privacy Policy. Your workshop results are saved there as you go, so they stay yours after the workshop ends, if you leave a cohort, or if you stop using the workshops. You decide what happens to what's in your vault.

AI features

Some parts of the workshops, such as your report and some reflections, are written with AI. Some of these features run through selfActual's AI platform. We don't use your workshop content to train AI models.

What Heliotrope Imaginal keeps

Heliotrope Imaginal keeps your workshop answers in the workshop app so the workshop can work: your progress, your Star Card, your reflections and your report. Deleting your Heliotrope Imaginal account doesn't delete your vault.
