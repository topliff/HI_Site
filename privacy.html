1. The Short Version

  • Your workshop answers, Star Card and reports are about you, and we treat them that way.
  • We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.
  • We don't use your answers to train AI models.
  • Your facilitator can see your progress, Star Card and reports. Section 6 says exactly what.
  • If you choose to create a selfActual vault, your copy of your results goes there and belongs to you.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, and ask us to delete it.

2. Who We Are

[Heliotrope Imaginal legal entity], based in Vancouver, British Columbia, runs the AllStarTeams and Imaginal Agility workshops at app2.heliotropeimaginal.com (the "Workshops"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and your choices. Contact: support@heliotropeimaginal.com. Our privacy officer is [name].

3. What We Collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Account: name, email, username, job title and organization if you give them, and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Workshop content: your assessment answers, Star Card, flow attributes, reflections, well-being ladder, future-self and growth-plan entries, final reflections, and the reports generated from them.
  • Messages: feedback, survey answers and emails to us.

3.2 Collected automatically

  • Usage: which steps you've completed, timestamps, browser, IP address.
  • Service logs: technical records of requests to our servers, used for security and debugging.
  • Cookies: a session cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

3.3 From the organization that invited you

If your employer, a professional association or another organization enrolled you, they may give us your name, email and the cohort or team you belong to.

4. How We Use It

  • To run the Workshops: your steps, your Star Card, your reports.
  • To generate AI reports and reflections from your answers (section 7).
  • To show your facilitator your progress and results (section 6).
  • To build team views for a cohort you belong to, such as the team matrix.
  • To support you and send service emails: invitations, password resets, reminders.
  • To keep the Workshops secure, improve them, and comply with the law.

We don't use your workshop content for advertising or to train AI models.

5. Sensitive Information

Your answers describe how you think, work and feel: strengths, flow, well-being and reflections. We use them only to run the Workshops for you and your cohort, as this policy describes. We don't use them to make decisions about you for anyone else, and we never sell them.

Workshop results are for development and self-reflection. They are not designed for hiring, firing, promotion or performance decisions, and organizations must not use them that way (Terms, section 6).

6. What Your Facilitator and Organization See

When you join a cohort, its facilitators can see:

  • your name, email and progress through each step;
  • your Star Card and flow attributes, including a downloadable copy;
  • your personal and professional AI reports;
  • where you sit in the team matrix for your cohort.

They can't see your password, and they can't sign in as you. Other participants in your cohort may see your Star Card in team activities. [Confirm whether the organization that paid for the cohort receives anything beyond what its facilitators can see.]

10. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • withdraw consent and ask us to delete your account and workshop content;
  • ask which facilitators and organizations can see your results.

Email support@heliotropeimaginal.com. We'll respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first.

In Canada, you can complain to our privacy officer first, then to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In the EU or UK, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In California, you have the rights to know, delete and correct, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. We don't sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms.

11. Children

The Workshops are for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. [Revisit if student cohorts run.]

12. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls by role, and backups, and we limit staff access to what's needed to run the Workshops. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we'll notify you and the regulator, as the law requires.
